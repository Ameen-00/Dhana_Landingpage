🎙 Voice-first · our difference

Most “AI lending” is a form with a chatbot. Dhana leads with the voice.

A borrower simply speaks, in Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil or English, and Dhana turns that conversation into a consent-gated, credit-ready file. The voice is the product, not a bolt-on.

RBI Digital Lending · 2025

RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025

LSP/RE accountability, consent before outreach, disciplined data practices. Dhana sits under the regulated entity. Not a shadow lender UX.

  • Consent before outbound voice / WhatsApp
  • Audit trail on soft bureau and AA fetches
  • HITL required on credit-relevant decisions
